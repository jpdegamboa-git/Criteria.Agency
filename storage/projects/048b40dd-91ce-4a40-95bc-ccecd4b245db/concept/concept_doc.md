## Concept Document — Demo Video

### Creative concept
A day in the life of a CTO, split-screen: left side shows chaos (meetings, emails, spreadsheets), right side shows calm (one dashboard, one flow, one decision). The split gradually merges as the product unifies their world.

### Creative north
"From chaos to clarity in 90 seconds."

### Tone definition
- Primary: Confident
- Secondary: Warm
- Avoid: Corporate-cold, salesy, generic
- Reference: "Apple product launch meets Slack brand film"
