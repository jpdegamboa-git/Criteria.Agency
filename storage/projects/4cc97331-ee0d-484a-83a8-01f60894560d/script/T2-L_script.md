### Key transitions
-   **Beat 1 → 2**: From abstract beauty of complexity to the visual jarring of inefficiency/stagnation.

-   **Beat 3 → 4**: Seamless flow from understanding the AI's role to seeing its tangible, predictive impact in action. Visuals become more precise and elegant.
-   **Beat 4 → 5**: Broader perspective shift from specific benefits to the overarching transformation and NovaTech's leadership.

### Non-negotiable moments
1.  The opening hook (first 15 seconds) MUST immediately establish the vastness of logistics and hint at the potential for chaos/inefficiency, drawing the viewer in with sophisticated visuals.
2.  The visualization of "predictive analytics" in Beat 4 must be compelling and intuitive, showing *how* issues are prevented, not just stating they are.
3.  The overall feeling must be one of elegant solution and confident mastery, never technical struggle or overwhelming data.
