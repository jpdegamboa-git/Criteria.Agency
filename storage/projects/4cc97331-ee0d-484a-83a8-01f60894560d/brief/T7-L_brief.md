## Communication Plan for NovaTech Solutions

This plan outlines T7-L's strategy for maintaining client confidence and clear communication throughout the project with NovaTech Solutions.

**Overall Strategy**: Proactive, clear, jargon-free, and professional communication. Anticipate Mr. Chen's need for efficiency and structured updates.

### 1. Initial Welcome & Onboarding (Mode 1)
*   **Action**: Sent welcome message, conducted smart onboarding questionnaire, gathered assets, calibrated expectations.
*   **Outcome**: Completed `onboarding_record.md` (above) for T1-L.
*   **Client View**: Received clear introductory email, understood initial requirements, provided necessary assets, confirmed understanding of process and scope.

### 2. Progress Communication (Mode 2)

**General Rules**:
*   All updates sent via email (as preferred for routine updates) with a clear call to action (if any) or simply informing of progress.
*   No timeline promises without PM confirmation. Use "within [n] business days" or "shortly" if a precise date isn't confirmed.
*   Bi-weekly calls will be scheduled proactively for major gate passages (G1, G2, G3, G4 review kickoff).

**Milestone Triggers & Communication (T7-L's internal plan)**:

*   **Trigger: G1 Pass (Concept Approved by PM)**
    *   **Internal Goal**: Confirm the overall creative direction.
    *   **Client Comms**: Email confirming concept approval, briefly outlining the approved direction, and informing that scriptwriting will begin. Announce the next step (script review) and its anticipated timeframe (e.g., "Script ready for your review in 5 business days").
    *   **Timing**: Within 4 hours of PM confirming G1 Pass.

*   **Trigger: G2 Pass (Script Approved by PM)**
    *   **Internal Goal**: Get client sign-off on the narrative and messaging.
    *   **Client Comms**: Email using the "Script approved" template. Prompt client to review the script in the portal. Inform them that visual planning (storyboard) is the next step and provide an estimated timeframe (e.g., "Storyboard ready in 7 business days"). Offer to schedule a quick call if they have any script questions *before* storyboard creation.
    *   **Timing**: Within 4 hours of PM confirming G2 Pass.

*   **Trigger: G3 Pass (Storyboard Approved by PM)**
    *   **Internal Goal**: Get client sign-off on visual flow and scene composition.
    *   **Client Comms**: Email using the "Storyboard approved" template. Inform them that production (generating video clips) has begun. Reassure them that no action is needed from them at this stage and that the first assembled cut will be ready for review within an estimated timeframe (e.g., "First cut ready in 10 business days").
    *   **Timing**: Within 4 hours of PM confirming G3 Pass.

*   **Trigger: G4 (First Cut available for client review)**
    *   **Internal Goal**: Present the first video assembly for major feedback.
    *   **Client Comms**: Email notifying them the first cut is in the portal. Explain the purpose of this review (focus on overall flow, pacing, and core message). Explain how to submit feedback via the portal. Confirm the deadline for their feedback (e.g., "Please submit your feedback within 3 business days"). Offer a call to walk through the cut if preferred.
    *   **Timing**: Within 2 hours of T6-L notifying T7-L that the first cut is published.

### 3. Feedback Processing (Mode 3)
*   **Trigger**: Client adds a comment on any deliverable in the portal.
*   **Internal Process**:
    1.  Read raw comment.
    2.  Diagnose feedback type (e.g., "The UI in scene 7 feels too static" -> Aesthetic/Pacing).
    3.  Clarify if vague (e.g., "Can you elaborate on what you mean by 'too static'? Do you want more animation, or a different camera movement?").
    4.  Translate into precise, actionable instruction for the relevant production team (e.g., "T5-L: Client requests more dynamic animation for the UI elements in Scene 7 (0:45-0:55). Consider subtle UI motion or a camera push-in to highlight data points.").
    5.  Check for scope impact.
    6.  Route translated instruction to team (T5-L for motion graphics, T6-L for editing, etc.).
*   **Client Comms**: Acknowledge receipt of feedback promptly (within 4-8 hours). Confirm understanding and explain the next steps (e.g., "Thanks for your detailed feedback on the first cut. We're now processing your notes and will apply them to the next revision. You can expect to see the revised cut in the portal within [PM-confirmed timeframe].").

### 4. Delivery & Approval (Mode 4)
*   **Trigger**: G5 Pass + delivery package received from T6-003.
*   **Internal Process**:
    1.  Verify `delivery_spec.md` for completeness.
    2.  Publish all final files (1080p, 4K, social cuts, with English and Spanish subtitles) to the client portal, clearly labelled.
    3.  Send delivery notification to client using the provided template.
    4.  Manage final approval, documenting explicit acceptance.
    5.  Handle post-G5 technical fixes (route to T6-003). Route creative changes to PM as new scope.
*   **Client Comms**: Professional delivery email with clear download instructions and post-delivery guidelines (technical vs. creative changes).

### 5. Scope Escalation (Mode 5)
*   **Trigger**: Client requests something outside original brief.
*   **Internal Process**:
    1.  Acknowledge request to client, state you will check impact (e.g., "I'll check what that would mean for the timeline and budget and get back to you.").
    2.  Document request precisely in `scope_escalation_{date}.md`.
    3.  Assess impact with PM (cost, timeline, pipeline).
    4.  Present options to client (add to scope, trade, defer).
    5.  Await client decision before communicating to production.
*   **Client Comms**: Transparent, professional communication of options, clearly outlining impacts.
