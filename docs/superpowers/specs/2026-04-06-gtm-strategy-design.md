# criteria.agency — GTM Strategy & Commercial MVP

> Date: April 6, 2026
> Status: Design spec
> Scope: Go-to-market strategy + Client Delivery Portal + Pricing & Checkout

---

## 1. Context

criteria.agency has comprehensive technical documentation (24 motors, 125 agents, 3 portals) but zero commercial strategy documentation. The platform's Video motor is the most technically advanced (20 agents designed, backend pipeline functional in mock mode), yet there is no frontend, no authentication, and no path to revenue defined.

The founder has:
- 20 years of video production experience
- Active paying clients in Washington, NY, Madrid, and Bogota
- Average project ticket of $1,000-$5,000 USD
- Solo operation (no team)
- $500-$2,000/month marketing budget
- 3-month timeline to first SaaS revenue via waitlist + private beta

This spec defines how criteria.agency goes from specification to revenue.

## 2. Positioning: "La IA genera. El criterio decide."

### Core Concept

criteria.agency does not sell AI. It sells 20 years of professional judgment codified into a system that knows when something is good, when it isn't, and why.

In a world where everyone has access to the same AI generation tools, **criterio (judgment)** is the only thing that can't be copied.

- The 5 quality gates are **puntos de criterio** — where the system decides if something deserves to exist
- The Showrunner is **automated creative judgment**
- The Critic is **the expert eye that never tires or settles**
- The 3+3 escalation rule is **the system admitting when it needs human criterio**

### 30-Second Pitch

> "Cualquiera puede generar un video con IA. Nosotros le ponemos criterio — 20 anos de experiencia en produccion codificados en un sistema que exige calidad profesional antes de entregar. La IA genera. El criterio decide."

### Narrative Expansion (phased messaging)

| Phase | Timeframe | Message |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| Launch | Month 1-3 | "Criterio aplicado a video profesional" |
| Expand | Month 4-6 | "Criterio aplicado a tu marca y contenido" |
| Full | Month 7-12 | "Criterio aplicado a todo tu marketing" |

### Positioning Against Competitors

| Competitor Type | Their Gap | criteria.agency Advantage |
|----------------|-----------|--------------------------|
| AI video tools (Runway, Sora, Kling) | Give you the brush, not the painter | Full production pipeline with professional judgment at every gate |
| Traditional agencies | Expensive, slow, bottlenecked by humans | Same criterio, fraction of time and cost |
| Marketing automation (HubSpot, Mailchimp) | Distribute content, don't create it | AI creates AND judges — full creative + distribution stack |
| Freelancer marketplaces (Fiverr, Upwork) | Individual talent, no system | Integrated system of specialized agents with quality gates |

### Messaging by Audience

| Audience | Pain Point | Message |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| SMB founder without team | "AI generates generic stuff, I can't tell if it's good" | "No necesitas saber de video. Nuestro criterio profesional garantiza que lo que recibes funciona." |
| Marketing manager (small team) | "We generate lots of AI content but quality is inconsistent" | "Deja de revisar todo tu. Nuestro sistema tiene el criterio para decidir que pasa y que no." |
| International company | "Need consistent quality without depending on one person" | "Criterio profesional escalable. Misma exigencia en cada entrega, sin importar volumen o mercado." |

## 4. Subscription Tiers

### 3-Tier Structure (Full Vision)

This table represents the complete tier structure as motors are activated. At launch, only Video is functional. Other motors show as "Coming soon" on the pricing page, creating anticipation and justifying future price increases.

| | **Starter** | **Pro** | **Enterprise** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Target** | Founder without team | Marketing manager, monthly volume | Multi-market company, full operation |
| **Video** (launch) | 1 project/month (up to 2 min) | 4 projects/month (up to 5 min) | Unlimited + premium formats (documentary, fiction) |
| **Brand Builder** (coming soon) | Basic Brand DNA (1 brand) | Complete Brand DNA + revisions | Multiple brands + active Brand Guardian |
| **Comunicar** (coming soon) | 2 channels, 10 posts/month | All channels, 50 posts/month | Unlimited + managed ads |
| **Entender** (coming soon) | Basic monthly reports | Full analytics + listeners | Complete intelligence + Opportunity Agent |
| **Vender** (coming soon) | 50 leads, basic CRM | 500 leads + automations | Unlimited + custom integrations |
| **Ads (Pauta)** | Not included | Not included (add-on) | Included with budget management |
| **Support** | Self-service + AI Copilot | Priority + AI Copilot | Dedicated Account Executive |
| **Price** | **$500/month** | **$2,000/month** | **Custom ($5,000+/month)** |

**At launch, the effective offering is:** Video production with AI + quality gates. The price is justified by video alone ($500/month for 1 professional video vs $1-5K per project at a traditional agency). Each new motor added post-launch increases value without increasing price — rewarding early adopters.

### Ads (Pauta) as Separate Add-On

Available to all tiers except included in Enterprise. Charged as management fee (% of ad spend or flat fee). Never mixed with base subscription. This protects margins and prevents a $500/month client from consuming $10K in ad management resources.

### Annual Pricing

2 months free on annual commitment (~17% permanent discount):
- Starter: $5,000/year (vs $6,000)
- Pro: $20,000/year (vs $24,000)

## 5. Conversion Funnel

```
WAITLIST -> FREE TRIAL (30 days) -> EARLY ADOPTER (3 months, 40% off) -> FULL PRICE
```

### Stage 1: Waitlist

- **Form captures:** Name, email, video type needed, company size
- **Automated welcome email** with link to a demo video produced by the platform
- **Nurture sequence:** 4 emails over 14 days showing capabilities, process, results
- **Goal:** 200-500 registrations in months 1-2

### Stage 2: Free Trial (30 days)

- Full access to chosen tier (Starter or Pro) for 30 days
- At launch, "full access" means Video motor (the only one active). As motors activate, trial scope expands automatically.
- Everything functional **except Pauta** (Ads requires payment from day 1)
- No credit card required to start (reduce friction)
- **1 video project included** in trial so they see real output
- At trial end: client has a professional video delivered + platform experience
- **Activation metric:** % of trials that complete their first video project (target: >60%)

### Stage 3: Early Adopter Discount (3 months)

- 40% discount on full price for 3 months
- Starter: $300/month (instead of $500)
- Pro: $1,200/month (instead of $2,000)
- Locked in — discount holds if they don't cancel during those 3 months
- Credit card required at activation
- **Conversion metric:** Trial-to-paid rate (target: >25%)

### Stage 4: Full Price

- Transition to regular pricing at month 5
- Annual payment option available (2 months free)
- **Retention metric:** Churn rate post-discount (target: <10%/month)

### Why This Funnel Works

1. Free trial eliminates the barrier — zero risk for the prospect
2. Delivering 1 video during trial creates real value — not an empty trial
3. 3-month discount creates urgency — "this price only exists now"
4. By the time full price hits, they depend on the service — high switching cost

## 6. Metrics & KPIs

### Funnel Metrics (monthly targets)

| Metric | Month 1 | Month 2 | Month 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Landing visitors | 1,000-2,000 | 2,000-4,000 | 3,000-5,000 |
| Waitlist signups | 100-200 | 200-400 | 300-500 |
| Trial activations | — | 20-40 | 40-80 |
| Trial-to-paid conversions | — | — | 10-20 |
| MRR | $0 | $0 | $3,000-$12,000 |

### Business Health KPIs

- **Activation rate:** % of trials completing first video project (target: >60%)
- **Trial-to-paid conversion:** % converting after 30 days (target: >25%)
- **Time-to-value:** Days from signup to first delivery (target: <7 days)
- **Trial NPS:** Satisfaction during free period (target: >40)
- **Post-discount churn:** % canceling after early adopter period (target: <10%/month)

### Product KPIs

- Brief-to-delivery time average
- Gate pass rate on first attempt
- Critic score average
- AI cost per project (to protect margins)

### North Star Metric

**If a client completes a video during trial and is satisfied, they pay.** The entire GTM optimizes for that moment to happen as quickly as possible.

## 8. Client Delivery Portal (Technical Spec)

### Purpose

Minimal portal where beta testers and paying clients receive deliverables, leave feedback, and approve work. This is NOT the full Client Portal (6 Spaces) — it's the smallest thing that enables the beta to work.

### Requirements

**Client experience:**
1. Client receives private link (email) to their project page
2. Project page shows: video player, project details, current status
3. Client can leave comments (text) on the deliverable
4. Client can approve or request revisions
5. Client can download final deliverable after approval

**Access model:**
- No account creation required during beta
- Private URL with unique token per project (e.g., `app.criteria.agency/review/{projectToken}`)
- Token expires after 30 days of inactivity
- No password — link IS the authentication for beta phase

**Project page structure:**
```
+------------------------------------------+
|  criteria.agency        [Project Name]   |
+------------------------------------------+
|                                          |
|           [ Video Player ]               |
|                                          |
+------------------------------------------+
|  Status: Awaiting Review                 |
|  Delivered: April 15, 2026               |
|  Version: 2 of 3                         |
+------------------------------------------+
|  Gate History:                           |
|  G2 (Script) - Passed                    |
|  G3 (Storyboard) - Passed               |
|  G5 (Final) - Awaiting client review     |
+------------------------------------------+
|  Comments:                               |
|  [Text input]              [Send]        |
|                                          |
|  - Client: "Love it, but logo at 0:45   |
|    is too small" (Apr 15, 3:20pm)        |
|  - criteria: "Fixed. New version ready." |
|    (Apr 15, 5:10pm)                      |
+------------------------------------------+
|  [ Approve & Download ]  [ Request Rev ] |
+------------------------------------------+
```

**Technical implementation:**
- Single Next.js page with dynamic route `/review/[token]`
- Video served from Bunny Stream (already in tech stack)
- Comments stored in database (new `comments` table: projectId, author, text, timestamp)
- Status transitions: `delivered` -> `in_review` -> `approved` | `revision_requested`
- Email notification to founder when client comments or approves
- Email notification to client when new version is uploaded

**Database additions:**
```
reviewTokens:
  - id (uuid)
  - projectId (FK -> projects)
  - token (unique string, 32 chars)
  - clientEmail (string)
  - expiresAt (timestamp)
  - createdAt (timestamp)

comments:
  - id (uuid)
  - projectId (FK -> projects)
  - author (string: "client" | "criteria")
  - text (string)
  - createdAt (timestamp)

deliveryStatus (add to projects table):
  - deliveryStatus (enum: draft | delivered | in_review | revision_requested | approved)
  - currentVersion (integer)
```

**What this is NOT:**
- Not the full Client Portal with 6 Spaces
- Not multi-tenant (all projects belong to one operator: the founder)
- Not authenticated beyond token-based links
- Not a dashboard — one page per project, no navigation

**Future path:** This review page becomes a component inside the Crear Space when the full Client Portal is built. The URL scheme, comment system, and approval flow are reusable.

## 9. Pricing & Checkout (Technical Spec)

### Purpose

Enable clients to view pricing, start a free trial, and convert to paid subscription. Uses Stripe for payment processing.

### Stripe Product Structure

```
Products:
  - criteria-starter
    - Price: $500/month (default)
    - Price: $300/month (early adopter, metadata: earlyAdopter=true)
    - Price: $5,000/year (annual)

  - criteria-pro
    - Price: $2,000/month (default)
    - Price: $1,200/month (early adopter, metadata: earlyAdopter=true)
    - Price: $20,000/year (annual)

  - criteria-enterprise
    - Custom pricing (handled manually, not through checkout)
```

### Trial Configuration

- Stripe trial_period_days: 30
- No payment method required during trial (Stripe supports this)
- Trial ends → client enters payment method → early adopter price activates
- If no payment method added → subscription canceled, access revoked

### Pricing Page

Single page at `criteria.agency/pricing` (or section in landing page):

```
+-----------------------------------------------+
|              Elige tu plan                      |
|   Prueba gratis por 30 dias. Sin tarjeta.      |
+-----------------------------------------------+
|                                                 |
|  STARTER          PRO            ENTERPRISE     |
|  $500/mes         $2,000/mes     Personalizado  |
|                                                 |
|  [Early Adopter]  [Early Adopter]               |
|  $300/mes x3      $1,200/mes x3                 |
|  primeros meses   primeros meses                |
|                                                 |
|  1 video/mes      4 videos/mes   Ilimitado      |
|  1 marca          3 marcas       Multi-marca    |
|  2 canales        Todos          Todos + pauta  |
|  50 leads         500 leads      Ilimitado      |
|  Self-service     Priority       Account Exec   |
|                                                 |
|  [Empezar gratis] [Empezar gratis] [Contactar] |
+-----------------------------------------------+
|  Pago anual: 2 meses gratis                    |
+-----------------------------------------------+
```

### Checkout Flow

```
1. Client clicks "Empezar gratis" on Starter or Pro
2. Form: name, email, company name
3. Stripe Checkout Session created (mode: subscription, trial_period_days: 30)
4. No card required during trial
5. Client redirected to confirmation page
6. Day 25: Email reminder "Tu trial termina en 5 dias"
7. Day 30: Email "Agrega tu metodo de pago para continuar"
8. Client adds card -> early adopter price ($300 or $1,200) activates
9. After 3 months: Stripe automatically transitions to full price ($500 or $2,000)
```

### Technical Implementation

- Stripe SDK (Node.js) integrated in Hono backend
- Webhook endpoint for: `customer.subscription.created`, `customer.subscription.updated`, `customer.subscription.deleted`, `invoice.payment_failed`
- Client record in database linked to Stripe customer ID
- Subscription status tracked: `trialing` | `active` | `past_due` | `canceled`
- Price transition from early adopter to full price handled via Stripe Subscription Schedules

**Database additions:**
```
Add to clients table:
  - stripeCustomerId (string, nullable)
  - subscriptionTier (enum: starter | pro | enterprise | null)
  - subscriptionStatus (enum: trialing | active | past_due | canceled | null)
  - trialEndsAt (timestamp, nullable)
  - earlyAdopterEndsAt (timestamp, nullable)
```

### Enterprise Tier

Not through Stripe Checkout. "Contactar" button opens email or calendar link. Enterprise deals are closed manually by the founder during beta/early phase.

### What This is NOT

- Not a full billing dashboard (client can't manage invoices, change plans in-app yet)
- Not multi-currency (USD only for launch — covers all 4 markets)
- Not self-service plan changes (handled manually for beta)

**Future path:** When the full Cuenta Space is built, Stripe Customer Portal handles plan changes, invoices, and payment methods. The Stripe product structure and webhook integration are reusable.

## 10. What Is NOT In This Spec

Explicitly excluded from this phase:

- Full Client Portal (6 Spaces) — replaced by minimal delivery portal
- Full Admin Portal (Mission Control) — founder operates via CLI/direct DB
- Multi-tenancy and tenant isolation — single operator (founder)
- Authentication system (Better Auth, MFA) — token-based links for clients
- Event Bus cross-motor communication — only Video motor active
- Any motor beyond Video — Brand Builder, Comunicar, etc. come after revenue
- Landing page design/copy — produced by the platform itself (dogfooding)
- Content marketing strategy — produced by the platform itself
- Ad campaigns (Pauta) — designed with the platform when ready
- SEO strategy — designed with the platform when ready
- Mobile app or responsive client portal

## 11. Relationship Between CriteriaFilms and criteria.agency

Both run in parallel with equal importance:

| | CriteriaFilms | criteria.agency |
|---|---|---|
| **What** | Video production service | SaaS marketing platform |
| **Revenue model** | Per-project ($1-5K) | Subscription ($500-$5K+/month) |
| **Role in GTM** | First client, case study, dogfooding | The product being sold |
| **During beta** | Founder operates platform for clients | Platform being validated |
| **Synergy** | Every CriteriaFilms project is a potential criteria.agency testimonial | criteria.agency powers CriteriaFilms production |

CriteriaFilms generates revenue while criteria.agency validates. They are not competing for resources — CriteriaFilms projects ARE criteria.agency validation.
